# Customization guide

Use this checklist with [Getting Started](GETTING_STARTED.md).

| Setting | What to change |
| --- | --- |
| Role and team | Describe your real responsibilities, including HR and non-HR work |
| Sources | Include only approved connectors that actually work |
| Timezone and dates | Use your local timezone and completed reporting periods |
| Work areas | Adapt Recruiting, Employee Support, Benefits, HR Operations, Admin, Leadership Support, etc. |
| Work nature | Keep Administrative / Operational / Strategic definitions consistent |
| Industry learning | Change biotech vocabulary to your own field or turn it off |
| Output | Choose HTML, private Notion, or chat-only output |
| Evidence | Require source links or explicit 'Needs verification' labels |

**Important:** Work distribution charts count **grouped items**, not hours or productivity. Do not invent business impact. Repeated policy questions are only **signals for human review**.

See [docs/customization.md](docs/customization.md) for more detail.
